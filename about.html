My Biography

My name is Bongile K'hethane. I was raised in Semonkong, a rural village in the Maseru District of Lesotho, famous for its mountains and the Maletsunyane Falls. Life in the countryside shaped me: I grew up close to nature and livestock, and from an early age I developed a deep love for animals.

My journey in education took me from the highlands of Semonkong to the National University of Lesotho in Roma, where I am a first-year Bachelor of Science (General) student. Moving from a rural village to university life was a big change, but it has taught me independence, discipline and the importance of working hard to achieve my goals.

Outside class I enjoy spending time with friends, such as Thato Motloheloa, who also comes from Semonkong. We often talk about home and encourage each other to keep going. My dream is to use my education to contribute to my community and to my country, Lesotho.

My Field of Study

I am studying a Bachelor of Science (General) in the Faculty of Science and Technology. This programme gives me a broad foundation in several sciences. This year I study Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Precalculus and Computer Applications, together with an academic communication course. It builds my problem-solving, analytical and practical skills and opens doors to careers in fields like biology, chemistry, environmental science, technology and teaching.
